// docs

Short answers.

Do you host my site?

No. You download a folder and host it yourself — Vercel, Netlify, GitHub Pages, a USB stick. We can't take it down because we never had it.

What's in the zip?

index.html, config.js, assets/pfp.png, assets/banner.png, README.txt. config.js holds the CA, the pair, your X link and the buy link. Change those, the page follows.

Where do the live numbers come from?

Public price APIs (DexScreener, GeckoTerminal fallback), refreshed every 30 seconds in the visitor's browser. No backend, no key, nothing to expire.

Why is it free?

Because $LAUNCHSITE pays for it. The creator tax on $LAUNCHSITE trades is routed to $LAUNCHSITE holders. The people who hold the coin are the people who fund the tool.

Can I edit the template?

It's HTML. Open it. Everything is in one file with the sections labelled.

Which launchpads?

Any that gives you a contract address. The buy button knows Pons, pump.fun and StonkFun; you can override it in config.js.
